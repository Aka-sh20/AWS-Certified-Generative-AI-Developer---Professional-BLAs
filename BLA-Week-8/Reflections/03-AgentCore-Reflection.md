# Reflection – Deploying and Managing AI Agents

This topic helped me understand what happens after an AI agent is designed.

Before this, I was mainly focused on the logic inside the agent. Now I understand that a real application also needs deployment, memory, security, monitoring, identity, policies, and human control.

AgentCore Runtime, Memory, and Gateway made the production side of agents easier for me to understand.

MCP was also interesting because it gives AI applications a more standard way to connect with external tools.

The Human-in-the-Loop section was important because it showed me that automation should not remove human judgment from every situation.

My main takeaway is that a useful production agent needs much more than a Foundation Model.
